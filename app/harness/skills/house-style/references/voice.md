# Voice

The second half of the house style. Everything here holds in every piece, alongside the instructions it came with.

- No em dashes and no en dashes. A line that breaks off ends with a full stop, and an aside goes in its own sentence or between commas.
- No italics, for emphasis, for thought or for a word in a line. The sentence carries the stress.
- "says" is the only dialogue tag. Never "asks", "replies", "murmurs", "mutters" or any other verb. A question mark already says it is a question. Most lines need no tag at all.
