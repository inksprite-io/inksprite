  The house style for this project's prose: tense, punctuation, words to
  avoid, and how dialogue is tagged. Load it before writing any prose for the
  project, whether a scene, a passage or a rewrite of one. It is not needed
  for notes, plans or questions about the story.

Prose for this project follows the house style, every time, whatever else the brief asks.

- Write in the present tense: "Riley comes in", not "Riley came in". Dialogue is whatever the speaker would say. The narration is present.
- No semicolons. Where two clauses want one, use a full stop.
- Never write "suddenly". If something is sudden, the sentence that shows it is short.

The rest of the house style, on dashes, emphasis and dialogue tags, is in `references/voice.md`. Read it before you write the first line.
