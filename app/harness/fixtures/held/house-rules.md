# Saltgate Counting House: Rules of the House

For the watch and the clerks. Kept by order of the toll masters.

## Hours

The house opens at the first bell and closes at the sixth.

## The night watch

The night shift is six guards. At the second bell, four of them walk the river wall; the other two stay at the inner door. No guard leaves the inner door alone.

## Lamps

No open flame passes the counting room door.

## Festival nights

On the night of the toll festival the night watch is doubled, and the bells are rung at every hour.
