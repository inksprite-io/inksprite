SALTGATE COUNTING HOUSE — RULES OF THE HOUSE
For the watch and the clerks. Kept by order of the toll masters.

1. Hours. The house opens at the first bell and closes at the sixth.

[p.2]
2. The night watch. The night shift is six guards. At the second bell, four of them walk the river wall; the other two stay at the inner door. No guard leaves the inner door alone.

3. Lamps. No open flame passes the counting room door.

[p.3]
4. Festival nights. On the night of the toll festival the night watch is doubled, and the bells are rung at every hour.
