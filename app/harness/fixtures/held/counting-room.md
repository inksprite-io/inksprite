The counting room was round, and every wall of it was drawers. Wren had expected a strongbox. Instead there were four hundred drawers of black oak, each with a brass number, and only one of them held the ledger.

The lock on the inner door had a dial of fourteen brass teeth. Osric turned it by ear while Wren held the lamp, and on the ninth tooth something inside the wall gave a soft, satisfied click.

"Drawer two hundred and six," Wren said. "My mother kept her accounts in sixes."
