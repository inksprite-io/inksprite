Wren slid off the onion barge where the river wall bent around the fish market. She counted the lanterns along the wall: nine, then a gap, then eleven. The gap was where the watch changed.

Osric was waiting under the bridge, as he had promised, with a coil of rope and a face that said he had been waiting too long.

"You're late," he said.

"The river was slow," said Wren, which was a lie, and they both knew it.
