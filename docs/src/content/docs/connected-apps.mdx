---
title: Connected Apps
description: Let another app on your computer, such as a coding agent, read and write the documents of the projects you choose, in the desktop app.
---

A connected app is another program on your computer that works in your
projects: it reads your documents, and edits and comments on them, as the
chat's model does. A coding agent is the usual one. Write a design in
inksprite, and the agent that builds it reads the design where you wrote it,
leaves its questions as comments, and puts the finished text in your
repository.

It works only in the [desktop app](/desktop/), and inksprite has to be open.
An app connects over MCP, so any app that can use an MCP server at an address
can be connected. This is the other way round from [MCP Servers](/mcp/),
which are services the chat's model calls.

## Connect an app

1. In **Settings** › **Connections** › **Connected apps**, click the plus
   beside the heading (**Add an app**). A card opens for it, named **Claude
   Code**.
2. Type its **Name** over that. Its comments and suggestions carry the name.
3. Under **Projects**, tick the projects it may work in. None is ticked to
   start with, and a project you make later isn't either.
4. Set the app up with what the card gives you. For Claude Code, click the
   copy button beside **Claude Code** and paste the command in a terminal. For
   any other app, give it the **Address**, and the **Token** as a bearer
   token (an `Authorization` header of `Bearer ` followed by the token).
5. Click **Done**.

The card says when the app last asked for anything.

:::note
On a Mac, an app running in a Docker container reaches your computer as
`host.docker.internal`. Put that in place of `127.0.0.1` in the address.
:::

## What an app can do

In the projects ticked for it, an app can list, read and search the documents.
Unless it is **Read only**, it can also write, move and delete them, and
comment on them. What it deletes goes to the
[trash](/projects/#delete-and-the-trash), and inksprite keeps a
[version](/editor/#versions) of a document from before each change.

A change shows in the document as it is made. To have its changes to a
document's text wait for you, ask the app's assistant for suggestions rather
than edits. They come as
[suggestions](/comments/#suggestions) in the text, under the app's name, for
you to accept or reject.

An app is given nothing else: no other project, no chats, no settings, and no
document you've [hidden from all chats](/chats/#hide-documents).

- **Read only**, on its card, lets it only list, read, search and export.
- **Remove** takes the connection away, and its token stops working at once.

## Put documents in a repository

Ask the app to export a document, a folder or the whole project. inksprite
makes the files ready and gives the app a link that works once, which the app
fetches into its own folder, laid out as the outline is, as
[Export](/projects/#export) writes them, without comments or suggestions.

Each Markdown file starts with a few lines saying which document it came from. They
tell whoever opens the file to change the document in inksprite and export it
again, and they let the app find the document later, whatever it has been
renamed to.

## The server

The card above your apps is the server they all connect to. **Status** says
whether it's running, and the switch beside it stops it for every app at once.
While it runs, **Reachable at** is the address to give an app.

The cog has its **Port**, 4657 unless you change it. After a change of port,
every app set up so far needs the new address.

inksprite listens only on your own computer.

## What ticking a project covers

The ticked projects and **Read only** apply to what an app asks inksprite for.
An app that runs on your computer as you, outside a container, can also open
the files on your disk without asking, your
[projects folder](/desktop/#your-projects-folder) among them. For an app in a
container, or one that can't otherwise read your files, the ticks are all it
can reach.
