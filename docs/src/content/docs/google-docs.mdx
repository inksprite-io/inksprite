---
title: Google Docs
description: Import Google Docs from your Drive into a project as Markdown documents you can edit, sync them with their Docs, and bring in other files you keep there.
---

Bring a draft you started in Google Docs into a project. Each Doc becomes a
document of its own, with its headings, lists and tables, ready to edit and
for the model to read. Sync it, and your edits go to the Doc and the Doc's
come back.

## Import from Drive

1. Right-click the project, or the folder to put the Docs in, and choose
   **Import** › **Google Drive…**.
2. Google's sign-in opens in a window of its own (in the desktop app, in your
   usual browser). Choose your Google account and allow inksprite to see the
   files you choose.
3. Google's file picker opens. Choose the Docs or other files to import; you
   can choose several at once.

inksprite puts them in the folder, and a message says how many it imported
and names any it couldn't. In the desktop app, the browser tab says you can
close it and go back to inksprite.

Google's picker opens each time you import, and inksprite can open only the
files you choose.

:::tip
If your browser blocks Google's window, inksprite says so. Click
**Try again**. If you close Google's window without choosing anything, click
**Cancel** in inksprite.
:::

Google's picker lets you open folders to find files, but choose the files
themselves: a folder chosen whole is skipped.

## What comes across

- **Google Docs** and **Word files** (`.docx`) become text documents, in
  Markdown: headings, lists, bold and italic, links, tables, horizontal
  lines and pictures. A drawing, or a picture in a table, is left out, and
  the message says how many.
- A Doc's **footnotes** come in at its end. Its **smart chips** come in as
  words: a person as their name, linked to their email address, and a date
  as it shows.
- A Doc with **suggested edits** still open comes in as it was before them,
  and the message names it. Accept them in Google Docs first to bring them
  in.
- **Google Sheets** come as a CSV file of the first sheet.
- **Google Slides** come as a PDF, and **Google Drawings** as a PNG image.
- **Other files**, such as PDFs, images and Markdown files, import as they
  would from your computer. See [PDFs, Images, and Ebooks](/files/).
- **Forms**, **Sites** and other Google files that can't be exported are
  skipped, and so is a Doc over Drive's 10 MB export limit.

Files on shared drives import too.

## Sync with the Doc

An imported Google Doc stays linked to its document. Sync it to send your
edits to the Doc and bring in the ones made there, by you or anyone you share
it with.

1. Click the sync button at the end of the editor's tabs, or right-click the
   document and choose **Google Doc** › **Sync**.
2. If inksprite isn't signed in to Google, Google's sign-in opens for the
   account the Doc came from, as it does for an import.

A message says when it's done. Edits on both sides come together, and in an
open document **Undo** takes back what the sync changed, in one step. Where
both sides changed the same words, inksprite shows the two side by side, with
the words that differ marked. Click the one to keep for each, then **Sync**.

A document imported before inksprite could sync, or brought back from a
backup, doesn't know how the Doc was when they last agreed. Its first sync asks
which to take as it is: **Keep mine** puts this document in the Doc, undoing
the edits made there since the import, and **Use the Doc's** takes the Doc,
losing the edits made here. A Doc with several tabs imported then can't sync:
import it again. A Word file from Drive isn't linked.

Some changes can't go to the Doc yet: footnotes added or taken out, a list
item's new kind or nesting, a heading inside a list, and rows or columns of a
table with merged cells. Sync says which and sends nothing; change it back, or
make the change in Google Docs. If the Doc has suggested edits open where the
document changed, accept or reject them in Google Docs first. Pictures you add
here stay here, and inksprite says so; pictures added in the Doc come in.
Comments stay where they were made.

The **Google Doc** submenu also has **Open in Google Docs**, and **Unlink…**,
which leaves the Doc as it is. To link it again, import the Doc again: it
arrives beside the first as **Name (2)**.

In a browser, a sign-in lasts an hour, and syncs within it don't ask again.
The desktop app stays signed in until you take its access away in your Google
account.

:::note
**Google Drive…** is on the menu at [inksprite.io](https://inksprite.io) and in
the [desktop app](/desktop/). A copy of inksprite you run yourself has it only
if it's set up with a Google project of its own.
:::
