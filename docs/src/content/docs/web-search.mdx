With web search on, the model can look things up while you chat: a date to
check, a source to read, a detail for a scene. It searches through a service
you connect, and reads the pages it finds.

## Choose a service

- **Exa** searches without a key, up to a limit Exa doesn't publish. A key
  from [Exa's dashboard](https://dashboard.exa.ai/api-keys) lifts the limit;
  [Exa's prices](https://exa.ai/pricing) say what searching with one costs.
- **Kagi** needs a key, from [Kagi's API page](https://kagi.com/api/keys). It
  charges for each search and each page it reads
  ([prices](https://kagi.com/api/pricing)).
- **Brave** works only in the [desktop app](/desktop/), and needs a key, from
  [Brave's API dashboard](https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/register). It
  charges for each search, with some free credit each month
  ([prices](https://brave.com/search/api/)).

## Set it up

1. Open **Settings** › **Connections**.
2. Under **Web search**, choose the service. In a browser, Brave is greyed out
   and marked **Desktop app only**.
3. Paste your key in **Key**, or leave it empty for Exa to search for free.
4. Click **Connect**. inksprite tries the service and says **Connected**, or
   why not. For Kagi and Brave, the check is one search, paid for like any
   other.
5. Under **Used in chats on**, switch on the [profiles](/chats/#profiles) whose
   chats should search. **Default** is on to start.

<Screenshot name="research-web-search" alt="Exa chosen under Web search, connected without a key, and on for chats on Default" />

One service is in use at a time. The others keep their keys, so you can switch
back without pasting them again. Choose **None** to stop every chat searching.

A [backup](/projects/#back-up-everything) leaves the keys out unless you tick
**Include API keys**. Restoring one without them names the services that need
a key again; paste it under **Key**.

## Choose which chats search

A chat searches when its profile is on under **Used in chats on**. To change
one chat, click the gear in the message box, open **Tools**, and switch **Web**
on or off. Its three tools, **Web search**, **Read web page** and **Save web
page**, can be switched off one at a time. The chat keeps your choice until you move it to another
profile; then it follows that profile.

**Web** is listed only while a service is chosen, with its key if it needs one.

## Ask

Ask for something that needs the web. To keep what it finds, say where:

> Search the web for where Yjs and Automerge stand today for rich text, and
> put what you find in Notes, with links.

The model searches, reads the pages that look useful, and answers; here, with a
note in the project and a link beside each finding. Click the tool calls above
its reply to see what it searched for, which pages it read, and what came back.

<Screenshot name="research-web-calls" alt="A note the model wrote from what it found on the web, and its 14 tool calls" />

The pages the model read stay in the conversation until a summary takes the
place of the turns that read them, so a follow-up about one doesn't read it
again. The [research guide](/research/) has this example in full.

The model reads PDFs and Word files on the web as well as pages. Exa and Kagi
read them for it; with Brave, the desktop app does.

## The model keeps what it finds

When the model finds a page or a document worth keeping, a source you'll want
or one it will come back to, it saves it to the project. It asks first: the
turn shows **Save** with the address and the folder it goes in.

- **Allow** saves this one.
- **Always allow** saves this one and every one after it without asking.
- **Deny** leaves it out, and the model is told so.

To be asked again after **Always allow**, switch on **Ask before saving
pages** in **Settings** › **Connections**, under **Web search**.

In the desktop app, a PDF or another file is saved as the file itself, as if
you had imported it. In a browser, most sites won't hand the file to the page,
so it's saved as its text. A page is saved as its text either way, with its
address and the day in its first line. The model doesn't save the same address
twice.

## Save a page to the project

To keep a page past a summary, or to read it yourself:

1. Open the tool calls and find the page, under `read_web_page`.
2. Click **Save to project**.
3. Change the **Title** if you like (it starts as the page's own), choose a
   **Folder**, and click **Save**.

The page becomes a document, whole, even if the model read only part of it.
Its first line gives the page's address and the day you saved it.

## When the free searches run out

When Exa's free searches run out, the model is told so, and says so in its
reply. Paste a key under **Key** in **Settings** › **Connections**, or search
again later.

## Privacy

Searches go straight from your computer to the service, with your key; they
don't pass through inksprite. The words searched for are the model's, drawn
from your chat. Exa and Kagi also read the pages for the model, so they see
which pages it reads. With Brave, the desktop app reads pages from their own
sites.

What the services say they keep:

- **Exa**: inksprite shows *Searches go to Exa, which may keep them.* under it.
  [Exa's privacy policy](https://exa.ai/privacy-policy) says it uses searches
  to train its models.
- **Kagi**: [its API terms](https://kagi.com/privacy/api) say searches may be
  logged for a while, apart from your account, and then deleted.
- **Brave**: [its privacy notice](https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/privacy-policy)
  says it keeps a record of searches for up to 90 days, for billing and
  troubleshooting.

[About](/about/) has the rest of inksprite's privacy notes.
