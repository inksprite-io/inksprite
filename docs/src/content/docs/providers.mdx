The chat needs a model, and a provider serves it. inksprite sends your requests
straight to the provider you choose; nothing goes through us.

- **[OpenRouter](/getting-started/#connect-an-ai-provider)** gives you one
  account for hundreds of models, paid as you go. Getting started walks through
  it.
- **[NanoGPT](#nanogpt)** is another account for many models, with a key or
  its own sign-in.
- **[Anthropic or OpenAI](#anthropic-and-openai)** take an API key from your
  account with them.
- **A [local model](#local-models)** runs on your own computer, free and
  private, if your computer is up to it.

The first time you use the chat, **Connect** above its message box sets one up:
choose the **Provider**, give it a key or an address, click **Connect**, then
choose a model. Add more in **Settings** › **Connections** › **AI providers**,
with the plus beside the heading (**Add connection**). Each connection there
is a card with its address; click one for its settings. A card says nothing
more while its connection answers, and **Key not accepted** or **Can't reach
it** when it last didn't.

## Anthropic and OpenAI

Make a key at [Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys) or at
[OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys). Then in inksprite:

1. Click **Connect** above the chat's message box, or **Add connection**, the
   plus in **Settings** › **Connections** › **AI providers**.
2. Choose **Anthropic** or **OpenAI** under **Provider**, paste the key in
   **API key**, and click **Connect**. It says *Connected*.
3. Click **Next** and choose a model.

## NanoGPT

Choose **NanoGPT** under **Provider**. Then either:

- paste a key from your NanoGPT account in **API key** and click **Connect**,
  or
- click **Sign in with NanoGPT**. A tab opens at NanoGPT; sign in or make an
  account there and approve inksprite, and the tab closes with the connection
  made.

:::note[Accounts made through the sign-in]
The sign-in carries inksprite's referral code. An account you make during it
counts as referred by inksprite, unless you untick the checkbox for that on
NanoGPT's consent screen.
:::

The [list of models](#models) shows NanoGPT's prices. inksprite asks NanoGPT
to cache Claude's prompts. Its speech
models can read for [narration](/narration/#choose-a-voice) too.

A NanoGPT connection's settings have **Zero data retention**, on to start
with. While it's on, every request through the connection, from chats, titles,
exploring, conversions and narration alike, asks NanoGPT for providers that
keep nothing. A model that none of them serves fails, saying *NanoGPT can't
serve* the model *with zero data retention*, and offers **Choose a model**;
switch it off to use such a model. Your NanoGPT key can have a data policy of
its own, set at NanoGPT. That holds as well, and inksprite can't see or change
it.

## Local models

**Provider** lists LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM and KoboldCpp, each at
the address it listens on when started without being told. For any other server
that speaks the OpenAI API, see [another server](#another-server).

1. Start your server as its section below says.
2. Choose it under **Provider**, change the **Address** if yours is elsewhere,
   paste the **API key** if it asks for one, and click **Connect**. inksprite
   asks the server what models it has.
3. Click **Next** and choose a model.

If it says *Can't reach it*, check that the server is running at that address.
In a browser, some servers also have to be told to let inksprite reach them;
their sections say how. The [desktop app](/desktop/) doesn't need that.

#### LM Studio

Download and load a model, as
[LM Studio's guide](https://lmstudio.ai/docs/app/basics) shows. Then
[start the server](https://lmstudio.ai/docs/developer/core/server) on the
**Developer** tab and turn on **Enable CORS** in its settings.

- **Address**: `http://localhost:1234/v1`
- **API key**: LM Studio's token, if you turned on **Require Authentication**

#### Ollama

[Install Ollama](https://ollama.com/download) and download a model with
`ollama pull`. To let inksprite reach it, set `OLLAMA_ORIGINS` to
`https://app.inksprite.io` and restart Ollama, as
[its FAQ](https://docs.ollama.com/faq#how-can-i-allow-additional-web-origins-to-access-ollama)
shows for each system.

- **Address**: `http://localhost:11434/v1`
- **API key**: none

#### llama.cpp

[Install llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/install.md)
and start its server with a model file, or with `-hf` and a model on Hugging
Face. [Its guide](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/master/tools/server)
has the other options.

```sh
llama-server -m model.gguf
```

- **Address**: `http://localhost:9931/v1`, or the address the server prints as
  it starts (older versions use port 8080)
- **API key**: what you gave `--api-key`, if anything

To turn a model's thinking off, set **Reasoning effort** to *Disabled* in the
chat: click the model's chip under the message box.

#### vLLM

[Install vLLM](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/getting_started/installation/)
and start its server with tool calls on, which the chat needs, and inksprite
allowed to reach it:

```sh
vllm serve Qwen/Qwen3-8B \
  --enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser hermes \
  --allowed-origins '["https://app.inksprite.io"]'
```

The parser depends on the model:
[vLLM's list](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/features/tool_calling/) says which
to use.

- **Address**: `http://localhost:8000/v1`
- **API key**: what you gave `--api-key`, if anything

#### KoboldCpp

[Download KoboldCpp](https://github.com/LostRuins/koboldcpp/releases/latest)
and start it with a model file:

```sh
koboldcpp --model model.gguf
```

- **Address**: `http://localhost:5001/v1`
- **API key**: what you gave `--password`, if anything

## Another server

For a server inksprite doesn't list, choose **OpenAI-compatible** under
**Provider**. Give it a **Name**, fill in the **Address** and **API key**, and
click **Connect**.

## Requests at once

Some things ask the model more than one question at a time: two skills in one
turn, or a conversion running while you chat. A connection's settings (click it
in **Settings** › **Connections** › **AI providers**) have **Requests at
once**, how many it is asked together.
Left empty, it is:

- **1** for a server on your own computer or your network, which works through
  one request at a time and keeps the last conversation it read ready for the
  next turn. llama.cpp is asked as many at once as it has slots (`--parallel`).
- **No limit** for OpenRouter, NanoGPT, Anthropic, OpenAI, vLLM, and a server
  elsewhere.
  When a provider says it is being asked too much, inksprite waits as long as
  it asks and tries again, asking less at once for a while.

What you're waiting on goes first: a turn or a skill never waits behind a
conversion.

## Models

**Settings** › **Models** lists the models of all your connections. **Other
models…**, under the model's chip in a chat's message box, opens it. First
come **Recent models**, the few that answered you lately, the ones that wrote
the most first; then **Recommended**; then each connection's models under its
name. Click a group's heading to shut it, and again to open it. Each model
says which connection serves it, so Sonnet on
OpenRouter and Sonnet on Anthropic are told apart, with its price per million
tokens read and written, how much it reads at once, and whether it has tool
support, where its connection says. Type to search. The filter in the search
narrows the list to some of your **Connections**, or to free models, and sorts
each connection's models by **Name** or **Cost**. The refresh beside it asks
the connections again. Anthropic's and OpenAI's prices come from OpenRouter's
list, which charges what they do.

With a chat showing, a click on a model moves the chat onto it, as choosing
it under the [chip in the chat](/chats/#the-chats-model) does. That also
starts the next new chats on the chat's [profile](/chats/#profiles) on it;
chats already going keep theirs.

The button at the end of a model's card (**Settings**) opens the model's
settings: **Generation**, **Sampling**, and on OpenRouter **Providers**. They
are the model's, so a change reaches every chat on it. Until you change one, a
model runs as its makers recommend, where OpenRouter lists that, and otherwise
as inksprite does by default; the reset beside a setting goes back to that.
**Generation** and **Sampling** show only the settings the model takes.

**Providers** says which of OpenRouter's providers serve the model first
(**Preferred providers**), whether it may **Fall back to others** when they
can't, and which it never goes to (**Ignored providers**). A recommended model
starts with the ones it runs best on.

A NanoGPT model has **Providers** too, where NanoGPT lets its providers be
chosen. Each provider is listed with its price as NanoGPT gives it. While the
connection's **Zero data retention** is on, only providers that keep nothing
are offered.

## When a reply fails

A reply that fails says why, and offers what fixes it where something does:

- A key that wasn't accepted, or a server that can't be reached, offers
  **Connection settings**.
- An OpenRouter or NanoGPT account out of credits offers **Add credits**.
- A model its service no longer offers gives **Choose a model**.
- On OpenRouter, *OpenRouter's provider routing leaves no provider for* the
  model means the connection's **Provider routing** (its **Ignored
  providers**, **Data collection** and **Quantization**) rules out every
  provider that serves it. **Connection settings** opens them.
- *None of the providers chosen for* the model *serve it* means the model's
  own **Providers** leave none, on OpenRouter or NanoGPT. Choose another
  model, or change them in the model's settings.
- On OpenRouter, a model that *can't use tools on OpenRouter* gives **Choose a
  model**.
