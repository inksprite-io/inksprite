A comment is a note on a passage: a reminder to yourself, or something for the
model to fix. The model sees your comments in the text, and can work through
them for you.

## Leave a comment

1. Select the passage in the editor.
2. Click **Comment** under the selection, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd>
   <kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> on Windows and
   Linux).
3. Type the comment and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line in the comment.
<kbd>Esc</kbd> or the cross (**Cancel**) puts it away unsaved.

The passage is highlighted in yellow. Hover over it to read the comment.

## Edit or resolve one

Click in a highlighted passage. The comment shows under the line, with two
buttons:

- The pencil (**Edit comment**) opens it to change. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd>
  <kbd>M</kbd> in the passage does the same. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click
  the tick (**Save**) to keep the change.
- The tick (**Resolve**) takes the comment off and leaves the passage as it
  is. That's also how you delete one.

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows and Linux)
brings back a comment you resolved in the editor.
Delete a commented passage and its comment goes with it.

A comment hides while you type in its passage, and shows again when you move
the caret.

## The Comments tab

Click **Comments** among the icons at the far left. It lists every comment in
the project, under the document each is in, in the outline's order. Each shows
the passage and the comment, a long one cut to a few lines.

- Click a comment to open its document at the passage and see all of it.
- Click a document's name to open it.
- Click the tick on a comment to resolve it. In a document that isn't open in
  a tab, that can't be undone.

The comment you picked, or the one the caret is in, is ringed in the list.

## In the text

A comment is kept in the document's text, in a form called CriticMarkup. You
see it in the [plain text editor](/editor/#the-plain-text-editor) and in a
downloaded document:

```
{==Make incoming changes visible.==}{>>k7m2qa: Out of scope for the first version. Move it to a Later section at the end.<<}
```

`{==…==}` holds the passage and `{>>…<<}` the comment, after the id the model
names it by. A new line in a comment is written `<br>`. The plain text editor
has no **Comment** button: there, a comment is its markup.

## On a phone

Select some text, and a **Comment** button comes up in a bar at the bottom of
the screen. Tap it, type the comment and tap the tick. On a touch screen,
Return starts a new line.

Tap in a highlighted passage to see its comment in the same bar, with the
pencil and the tick.

**Comments** is in the **⋯** menu on the top bar. Pick a comment there to go
to it in the editor.

## Ask the model

In a chat with the **Documents** tools on, as on the **Default** profile, the
model sees your comments when it reads a document, and a listing of the
project says how many each document has. Ask it to deal with them, and it:

- lists them, to find them all
- rewrites a passage to answer its comment, which resolves the comment
- resolves a comment that needs no change, or that it answered in the chat

Any change to a passage's words resolves its comment. When the model rewrites
a whole document, a comment stays if its words are still there unchanged. If some comments are notes to yourself, say
which ones are for the model.

### An example

The [research guide](/research/#leave-comments) leaves three comments on a
design doc, the ones in the Comments tab above, then asks in the chat:

> I left three comments on the design doc. Work through them, and resolve each
> one when it's done.

The model lists the comments, edits the doc for each, and says what it did.
All three are resolved, and the Comments tab is empty. Click the tool calls
above its reply to see each change it made.
