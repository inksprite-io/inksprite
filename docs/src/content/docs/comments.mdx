A comment is a note on a passage: a reminder to yourself, or something for the
model to fix. The model sees your comments, can work through them for you,
answer them, and leave comments of its own.

## Leave a comment

1. Select the passage in the editor.
2. Click **Comment** under the selection, right-click it and choose
   **Comment…**, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd>
   (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> on Windows and Linux).
3. The **Comments** tab opens beside the text, with a new comment in its
   place among the document's, by where the passage is. Type it and press
   <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

The passage stays tinted while you write. <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>
starts a new line in the comment, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the cross
(**Cancel**) puts it away unsaved.

The passage is highlighted in yellow. Hover over it to read the comment.

Comments can overlap. Where they do, the yellow is deeper, hovering shows
each comment, and clicking picks the one on fewer words.

## Reply, edit or resolve

A comment is read and answered in the **Comments** tab: the passage at the
top with the tick (**Resolve**), then everything said on it, each under the
name of who wrote it, **You** or **Assistant**. Click in a highlighted passage
and its comment is picked in the tab, ringed, with a field to reply.
<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> in the passage, or **Reply to
comment…** in its right-click menu, opens the tab there with the field
ready.

- Type in the field and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to reply.
- Hover over something said and click its dots for **Edit** (anything you
  said) and **Delete**. Deleting the first one deletes the comment; deleting
  a reply deletes just that reply.
- The tick (**Resolve**) takes the comment off the passage and leaves the
  passage as it is. The comment is kept with the resolved ones in the
  Comments tab.

<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows and Linux)
brings back a comment you resolved in the editor. It doesn't take back a
comment you've just made, or one you deleted.

Delete a commented passage and its comment stays in the Comments tab, struck
through, until you resolve it. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> brings the passage
back with its comment on it.

A comment hides while you type in its passage, and shows again when you move
the caret.

## The Comments tab

Click **Comments** among the icons at the far left. It lists every open
comment in the project, under the document each is in, in the outline's order.
Each shows the passage and everything that's been said on it. A comment whose passage was deleted comes after the others, its
passage struck through.

- Click a comment to open its document at the passage and reply under it.
  Putting the caret in a commented passage picks its comment in the list the
  same way.
- Click a document's name to open it.
- Click the tick on a comment to resolve it, or the dots on something said to
  edit or delete it.

The comment you picked, or the one the caret is in, is ringed in the list.

The clock at the top (**Show resolved comments**) lists the resolved ones too.
The circular arrow (**Reopen**) in place of the tick puts one back on its
passage, if its words are still in the text, and **Delete** on its first
post's dots deletes it for good.

## In the text

Where a comment is on the passage is kept in the document's text, in a form
called CriticMarkup. You see it in the
[plain text editor](/editor/#the-plain-text-editor) and in a downloaded
document:

```
{==Make incoming changes visible.==}{>>k7m2qa<<}
```

`{==…==}` holds the passage and `{>>…<<}` the comment's id. A comment on
words inside another's sits inside its markup. Where two only partly overlap,
one of them is written in two pieces with the same id.

What was said, by
whom, and the replies are kept with the document, apart from its text: in the
desktop app, at the top of the document's file. The plain text editor has no
**Comment** button: there, a comment is its markup.

## On a phone

Select some text, and a **Comment** button comes up in a bar at the bottom of
the screen. Tap it, type the comment and tap the tick. On a touch screen,
Return starts a new line.

Tap in a highlighted passage to see its comment in the same bar, and tap the
comment to reply.

**Comments** is in the **⋯** menu on the top bar. Pick a comment there to go
to it in the editor.

## Ask the model

In a chat with the **Documents** tools on, as on the **Default** profile, the
model sees the open comments when it reads a document, after the text, and a
listing of the project says how many each document has. Ask it to deal with
them, and it:

- lists them, to find them all
- rewrites a passage to answer its comment, which resolves the comment
- replies to a comment, under it, to say what it did or to ask what you meant
- resolves a comment that needs no change, or that it has answered

Any change to a passage's words resolves its comment. When the model rewrites
a whole document, a comment stays if its words are still there unchanged. A
resolved comment is kept, so **Reopen** brings back a note to yourself that
the model rewrote. If some comments are notes to yourself, say which ones are
for the model.

The model also leaves comments of its own: ask for notes on a chapter and it
puts each where it belongs, and it marks a gap it couldn't fill in what it
writes.

### An example

The [research guide](/research/#leave-comments) leaves three comments on a
design doc, the ones in the Comments tab above, then asks in the chat:

> I left three comments on the design doc. Work through them, and resolve each
> one when it's done.

The model lists the comments, edits the doc for each, and says what it did.
All three are resolved, and the Comments tab has none open. Click the tool calls
above its reply to see each change it made.
