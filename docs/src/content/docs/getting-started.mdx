inksprite is a writing app with an AI you can brainstorm with. It runs in your
browser at [inksprite.io](https://inksprite.io), or as a
[desktop app](https://github.com/inksprite-io/inksprite/releases/latest) for
macOS, Windows and Linux. There's no account to make.

## Open inksprite

The first time you open inksprite, a welcome window opens. Connect an AI
provider on its **AI** tab now, or [later](#connect-an-ai-provider).

<Screenshot name="welcome" alt="The welcome dialog" />

:::caution[Your work stays on your computer]
In a browser, your projects are kept in that browser's storage and nowhere
else. Clearing the site's data deletes them, and Safari clears it after a week
without a visit. [Back up](#back-up-your-work) now and then, or use the desktop
app, which no browser can clear.
:::

## Start a project

A project is a set of documents, kept in folders the way you like.

<Clip name="new-project" label="Creating a project and its first document" />

1. Click **New project**, give it a title and click **Create**.
2. Click **New document**, type its name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Write. Everything saves as you type.

The editor formats Markdown as you type: `#` and a space starts a heading,
`**bold**` and `*italic*` format when you close them, `-` or `1.` and a space
start a list, and `>` a quote. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd>
(<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd> on Windows and Linux) for every shortcut.

<Screenshot name="workspace" alt="The outline, the editor and the chat" />

The **outline** on the left lists the project's documents, the **editor** in
the middle has a tab for each open one, and the **chat** is on the right. The
icons at the far left switch the sidebar between the outline, your chats,
[narration](/narration/) and comments. **Settings** is at the bottom.

### Folders

Right-click the project, or any folder in it, to add a document or a folder.
Drag documents to move them.

A folder sorts its documents by title. To put them in your own order, choose
**Keep in order** from the folder's menu, then drag them into place.

<Screenshot name="outline-menu" alt="The menu for a folder" />

## Connect an AI provider

The chat needs a model to talk to. inksprite sends your requests straight from
your computer to the provider you choose; nothing goes through us.

- **OpenRouter** gives you one account for hundreds of models, paid as you go.
  It's the quickest to set up.
- **[Anthropic or OpenAI](#anthropic-and-openai)** take an API key from your
  account with them.
- **A [local model](#local-models)** runs on your own computer, free and
  private, if your computer is up to it.

### OpenRouter

Click **Connect** in the chat panel, then **Connect to OpenRouter**.

<Screenshot name="connect-provider" alt="Connect an AI provider" />

A tab opens at OpenRouter. Make an account there, or click **Sign in** at the
bottom if you have one.

<Screenshot name="openrouter-sign-up" alt="OpenRouter's sign-up page" />

Then click **Authorize**. The tab closes and inksprite says it's connected.

<Clip name="connect-openrouter" label="Connecting to OpenRouter" />

:::note
A new connection starts on a paid model. Add
[credits on OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits), or
[choose](#choose-a-model) a free model: its name ends in *(free)*.
:::

### Choose a model

Open **Settings** (the gear at the bottom left) and choose **AI**. The model
you pick here is the one your chats use.

<Screenshot name="ai-settings" alt="The AI settings" />

Click **Model** to see what the provider offers, and type to search the list.

<Screenshot name="choose-model" alt="Choosing a model" />

**Generation** and **Sampling** show only the settings the chosen model takes.

### Anthropic and OpenAI

Make a key at [Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys) or at
[OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys). Then in inksprite:

1. Open **Settings** › **AI**, choose **Anthropic** or **OpenAI** under
   **Provider**, and click the **⋮** beside it.
2. Paste the key in **API key** and click **Connect**. inksprite says how
   many models it found.
3. Close the provider's window and [choose a **Model**](#choose-a-model).

### Local models

inksprite knows LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM and KoboldCpp, and works
with any other server that speaks the OpenAI API.

1. Start your server as its section below says.
2. Open **Settings** › **AI** and choose the server under **Provider**.
3. Choose a **Model**. inksprite asks the server what it has.

In a browser, some servers have to be told to let inksprite reach them; their
sections say how. The desktop app doesn't need that.

If no models come, or your server is at another address or asks for a key,
click the **⋮** beside **Provider**, change the **Endpoint** or paste the
**API key**, and click **Connect**.

<Clip name="connect-lmstudio" label="Connecting to LM Studio" />

#### LM Studio

Download and load a model, as
[LM Studio's guide](https://lmstudio.ai/docs/app/basics) shows. Then
[start the server](https://lmstudio.ai/docs/developer/core/server) on the
**Developer** tab and turn on **Enable CORS** in its settings.

- **Endpoint**: `http://localhost:1234/v1`
- **API key**: LM Studio's token, if you turned on **Require Authentication**

#### Ollama

[Install Ollama](https://ollama.com/download) and download a model with
`ollama pull`. To let inksprite reach it, set `OLLAMA_ORIGINS` to
`https://inksprite.io` and restart Ollama, as
[its FAQ](https://docs.ollama.com/faq#how-can-i-allow-additional-web-origins-to-access-ollama)
shows for each system.

- **Endpoint**: `http://localhost:11434/v1`
- **API key**: none

#### llama.cpp

[Install llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/install.md)
and start its server with a model file, or with `-hf` and a model on Hugging
Face. [Its guide](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/master/tools/server)
has the other options.

```sh
llama-server -m model.gguf
```

- **Endpoint**: `http://localhost:9931/v1`, or the address the server prints as
  it starts (older versions use port 8080)
- **API key**: what you gave `--api-key`, if anything

To turn a model's thinking off, set **Reasoning effort** to *Disabled*.

#### vLLM

[Install vLLM](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/getting_started/installation/)
and start its server with tool calls on, which the chat needs, and inksprite
allowed to reach it:

```sh
vllm serve Qwen/Qwen3-8B \
  --enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser hermes \
  --allowed-origins '["https://inksprite.io"]'
```

The parser depends on the model: [vLLM's list](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/features/tool_calling/)
says which to use.

- **Endpoint**: `http://localhost:8000/v1`
- **API key**: what you gave `--api-key`, if anything

#### KoboldCpp

[Download KoboldCpp](https://github.com/LostRuins/koboldcpp/releases/latest)
and start it with a model file:

```sh
koboldcpp --model model.gguf
```

- **Endpoint**: `http://localhost:5001/v1`
- **API key**: what you gave `--password`, if anything

## Your first chat

Type in the box at the bottom of the chat and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>
(<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line). The model can read,
search and write the project's documents: ask, and it does.

<Clip name="first-chat" label="A first chat" />

To see what the model read and wrote, click the tool calls above its reply. A
document it writes shows in the outline at once.

<Screenshot name="first-chat" alt="A chat that wrote a document" />

## Back up your work

Open **Settings** › **System** and click **Export backup**. The file holds
every project, chat and setting. Leave **Include API keys** off unless the file
stays with you.

To restore a backup, or move to another browser or the desktop app, click
**Import backup** there. It replaces everything inksprite holds with the
backup.

<Screenshot name="backup" alt="Exporting a backup" />
