inksprite is a writing app with an AI you can brainstorm with. It runs in your
browser at [inksprite.io](https://inksprite.io), or as a
[desktop app](/desktop/) for macOS, Windows and Linux. There's no account to
make, unless you want to [sync](/sync/) your work between devices.

## Open inksprite

The first time you open inksprite, a welcome window opens. You can connect an
AI provider on its **AI** tab now, or [later](#connect-an-ai-provider). Click
**Start writing** to close it.

If you'd rather write without AI, click **turn them off** on the welcome, or
later turn off **Enable AI** in **Settings** › **System**. The chat and the
narration go away until you turn it back on, and any chats you had are kept.

<Screenshot name="welcome" alt="The welcome window" />

:::caution[Your work stays on your computer]
In a browser, your projects live only in that browser's storage, unless you
[sync](/sync/) them. Clearing the site's data deletes them, and Safari clears
it after a week without a visit unless you
[install inksprite](#install-it-from-your-browser) on an iPhone or iPad.
[Back up](#back-up-your-work) now and then, or use the desktop app, which no
browser can clear.
:::

### Install it from your browser

You can install inksprite from your browser, as an app with an icon and a
window of its own. It opens without a connection too, though the AI still
needs one.

- **Chrome or Edge**, on a computer or a Chromebook: click the install button
  at the end of the address bar. The app has the same projects as the
  browser's tabs.
- **iPhone or iPad**: in Safari, add inksprite to your
  [Home Screen](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/bookmark-a-website-iph42ab2f3a7/ios#iph4f9a47bbc).
  Safari doesn't clear the Home Screen app's data after a week, as it does a
  tab's. The app starts empty, though, because it doesn't share Safari's
  storage. To bring your projects over, [back up](#back-up-your-work) in
  Safari and restore the backup in the app, or [sync](/sync/) both.
- **Android**: in Chrome, open the menu, choose **Add to Home screen**, then
  **Install**. The app has the same projects as the browser's tabs.

When there's a new version, inksprite says so. Click **Reload** to start it.

## Start a project

A project is a set of documents, kept in folders the way you like.

<Clip name="new-project" label="Creating a project and its first document" />

1. Click **New project**, give it a title and click **Create**.
2. Click **New document**, type its name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Write. Everything saves as you type.

The editor formats Markdown as you type: `#` and a space starts a heading,
`**bold**` and `*italic*` format when you close them, and `-` or `1.` and a
space start a list. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>/</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
<kbd>/</kbd> on Windows and Linux) for every shortcut, or see
[the editor](/editor/).

<Screenshot name="workspace" alt="The outline, the editor and the chat" />

The **outline** on the left lists the project's documents, the **editor** in
the middle has a tab for each open one, and the **chat** is on the right. The
icons at the far left switch the sidebar between the outline, your chats,
[narration](/narration/) and comments. **Settings** is the gear at the bottom.

### Folders

Right-click the project, or a folder in it, to add a document or a folder.
Drag documents to move them. A folder sorts its documents by name; to order
them yourself, choose **Keep in order** from its menu and drag them into
place. See [Projects](/projects/#arrange-the-outline) for more.

<Screenshot name="outline-menu" alt="The menu for a folder" />

## Connect an AI provider

The chat needs a model to talk to. This guide uses OpenRouter, which gives you
one account for hundreds of models, paid as you go. For Anthropic, OpenAI, or a
model on your own computer, see [AI Providers](/providers/).

Click **Connect** above the chat's message box, then **Connect to OpenRouter**.

<Screenshot name="connect-provider" alt="The Connect an AI provider dialog" />

A tab opens at OpenRouter. Make an account there, or click **Sign in** at the
bottom if you have one.

<Screenshot name="openrouter-sign-up" alt="OpenRouter's sign-up page" />

Then click **Authorize**. The tab closes and inksprite says it's connected.

<Clip name="connect-openrouter" label="Connecting to OpenRouter" />

:::note
A new connection starts on a paid model. Add
[credits on OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits), or
[choose](#choose-a-model) a free model: its name ends in *(free)*.
:::

### Choose a model

Open **Settings** › **AI**. The model you pick here is the one your chats use.

<Screenshot name="ai-settings" alt="The AI settings" />

Click **Model** to see what the provider offers, and type to search the list.

<Screenshot name="choose-model" alt="Choosing a model" />

## Your first chat

Type in the box at the bottom of the chat and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>
(<kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line). The model can read,
search and write the project's documents: ask, and it does.

<Clip name="first-chat" label="A first chat" />

To see what the model read and wrote, click the tool calls above its reply. A
document it writes shows in the outline at once.

<Screenshot name="first-chat" alt="A chat that wrote a document" />

## Back up your work

Open **Settings** › **Sync and sharing** › **Backup** and click **Export**. Leave
everything ticked and **Include API keys** off unless the file stays with you,
and click **Export**. The file holds every project, chat and setting.

<Screenshot name="backup" alt="The Export dialog" />

To restore it, or move to another browser or the desktop app, see
[Projects](/projects/#back-up-everything).
